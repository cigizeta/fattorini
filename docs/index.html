Inside Fattorini

Fattorini is a chess engine: a program that plays chess, talking to chess interfaces through the UCI protocol. It is written in Rust, and its neural networks are trained on a limited compute budget. It is named after Antonio Fattorini, an Italian immigrant to Yorkshire whose family's business is credited with making the first chess clock in 1883.

This site is a small chess programming wiki, restricted to the techniques Fattorini uses, each explained in plain terms first, then precisely, with a formula or a little pseudocode.
